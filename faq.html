Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before placing a sweet little order.

How far ahead should I order?

We recommend at least one week for standard shop items, two weeks for custom cakes, and four to six weeks for tiered or wedding cakes. Rush availability varies.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Yes. Bakery pickup is free. Local delivery is available within the delivery area for a fee that should be configured before launch.

Can you accommodate allergies?

Some substitutions may be possible, but our kitchen handles wheat, dairy, eggs, soy, peanuts, and tree nuts. We cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.

How do custom cake orders work?

Complete the custom cake inquiry. We’ll follow up with design details and a quote. Your date is reserved after the deposit is paid through your future payment system.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Changes depend on timing and ingredient availability. The sample policy in our terms should be customized to match your real business practices.

Is the online checkout live?

No. This package includes a front-end checkout demonstration only. It stores the cart in the browser, does not send personal data, and does not process payments.
