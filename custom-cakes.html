How it works

From idea to cake table

Custom cakes begin at $68 and are priced according to servings, flavour, decoration, and delivery. We recommend inquiring at least two weeks before your event; four to six weeks is best for wedding and tiered cakes.

  • Choose cake, filling, and buttercream flavours
  • Share colours, inspiration, and serving count
  • Receive a design summary and quote
  • Confirm with a deposit through your future payment provider
