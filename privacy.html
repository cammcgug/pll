Information we collect

When the real contact, inquiry, or checkout features are connected, Pretty Little Layers may collect information you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, event details, order preferences, and messages.

How we use information

We may use information to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, fulfill orders, provide customer support, send service communications, improve the website, and comply with legal obligations. Marketing messages should be sent only when consent is provided and must include a way to unsubscribe.

Payments

This demonstration website does not collect payment details. If a payment provider is connected later, payment card information should be handled directly by that provider under its own privacy terms.

Local browser storage

The demo shop stores cart contents in your browser using localStorage. This information stays on the device unless browser data is cleared. No analytics or advertising cookies are included in this package.

Sharing

Information should be shared only with service providers needed to operate the business—such as hosting, email, delivery, and payment services—or when required by law. Pretty Little Layers should not sell personal information.

Retention and security

Keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. Use appropriate safeguards and limit access to people who need it.
